Hi, I'm Zaki

I'm an ethnomusicologist, experimental musician, and creative technologist completing my undergraduate degree at Florida Gulf Coast University. My work explores universal patterns in musical improvisation, experimental sound practices, and the creative applications of emerging technology.

Recently, I defended my honors thesis examining temporal consciousness across jazz, Arabic maqam, and Javanese gamelan traditions—work that earned distinction and opened pathways toward deeper research. Alongside academics, I maintain an active creative practice with 20+ released albums under Aspen Da'nee / ScilentSymphony, exploring generative systems, audio-reactive installations, and AI-assisted workflows.

What drives me is the space between disciplines: where ethnomusicology meets experimental sound art, where academic rigor intersects with technological innovation, where traditional improvisation theory illuminates contemporary creative practice. I believe the most interesting work happens at these intersections.
