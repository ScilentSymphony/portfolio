Ben - A Literary Novel Series

Multi-Book Literary Fiction • In Development • 2020-Present

Ambitious literary project exploring trauma, healing, and identity through experimental narrative structure. The series follows protagonist Ben across 20 years (ages 1-20), examining generational trauma, cycles of abuse, and the journey toward self-understanding.

  • 176+ detailed plot sequences across four-book series
  • Complex multi-POV narrative structure organized by emotional themes
  • Extensive world-building and character development
  • Integration of prose, poetry, and experimental forms
  • Deep psychological research on attachment theory and trauma processing

Development Status: Extensive plotting, character development, setting construction, and thematic research completed. Currently able to narrate substantial sections with detailed structural documentation.
